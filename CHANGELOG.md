# Changelog

## 1.0 (0)

First public prerelease for testing and bug reports.

- None of the ads and analytics frameworks in Kick's app.
- Rewind live streams to the start and jump back to live.
- Picture in Picture, background audio, AirPlay and a mini player.
- Sleep timer, VOD downloads to Files and clips saved to Photos.
- 7TV emotes, live emote updates, badges and name colors. Optional BTTV and FFZ.
- Chat history without duplicates and chat replay for VODs.
- Block words and people. Hide mature streams, bot commands, repeated messages and sub alerts.
- Follows and saved clips stay on your device. Import your follows from Kick.
- Sign in on kick.com's own page to chat and see your Drops progress.
- Carbon and Pure Black themes with accent colors and seven app icons.
- Tweaks for chat size, quality, skip length and the opening screen.

### Known issues

- Watching here does not earn crate rewards.
- Watching here might not count toward Drops or level badges.
- The Amazon IVS player sends playback quality data to Amazon.
